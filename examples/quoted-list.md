# A list inside a quote

A list can sit inside a block quote. Each line keeps the quote's `>`
and the list's own marker after it:

> Before you leave:
>
> - lock the back door
> - water the plants
>     - the ferns twice
> - [ ] take the bins out
> - [x] pack the charger
>
> 1. first
> 2. second

Off the cursor's line, the `>` becomes the quote bar and the markers
become bullets and task boxes. Move the cursor onto a line to see its
raw Markdown.

In Insert mode, `Enter` at the end of a quoted item starts the next
one, with both the `>` and the marker.
